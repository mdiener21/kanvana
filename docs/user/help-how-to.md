# Help

The same help as the **Help** item in the app's controls menu (`client/src/index.html`, `#help-modal`).

## Getting around

- **Controls:** Click ⋮ in the header to open the controls panel: switch boards, swim lanes, Add Column, Manage Boards, Manage Labels, Settings, Reports, Calendar and dark mode. On a phone, tap **Controls** in the bottom bar; swipe the bar for Reports, Calendar, Dark mode and Help.
- **Search:** Type in the search box to filter tasks by title, description, label or label group.
- **Keyboard:** `Ctrl+B` opens Manage Boards; `Esc` closes dialogs and the controls panel. See [keyboard shortcuts](keybindings.md).

## Tasks

- **Add:** Click **+** in a column header.
- **Edit:** Click a card to change its title, description, priority, due date, labels, sub-tasks and links to other tasks.
- **Move:** Drag a card to another column, or to another lane when swim lanes are on.
- **Delete:** Click the bin icon on the card.

## Columns

- **Add:** Use **Add Column** in the controls panel.
- **Reorder:** Drag a column by its grip handle; click its collapse icon to fold it away.
- **Edit, sort, delete:** Use the column's ⋮ menu. Done and the last remaining column can't be deleted.

## Boards & labels

- **Boards:** In **Manage Boards**, create a board (blank or from a template), rename, export, import or delete it. See [boards](boards.md).
- **Labels:** In **Manage Labels**, create, colour and group labels (e.g. People, Status). Label search matches names and groups. See [labels](labels.md).
- **Swim lanes:** Turn them on in the controls panel to group rows by label, label group or priority; choose the grouping and lane order in **Settings**.

## More views

- **Time tracking:** Click the clock icon in the header. Press `?` there to see its shortcuts.
- **Reports & Calendar:** Open them from the controls panel.
- **Notifications:** The bell lists tasks that are due soon or overdue; set how far ahead to warn you in **Settings**.

## Your data

- Everything is saved in this browser and works offline. Use **Manage Boards → Export** to keep a backup file.
- To use the same boards on other devices, click **Go Online** and sign in. Sync is optional.
